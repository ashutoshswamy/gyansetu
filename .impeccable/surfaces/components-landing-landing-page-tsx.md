# Surface brief: landing page (/)

Scope: app/page.tsx landing. Mode: Persuade. Audience: prospective volunteers (primary); institutions, sponsors, alumni secondary. Action: Apply for a Visit (/sign-up, or /dashboard when signed in). Proof: real field photos (public/posts, photo on top ~75%, caption band below), four real stats, approved testimonials. Constraints: keep logo, copy facts, section order, anchors #our-story #how-it-works #testimonials #alumni #institutions, routes. Full overhaul of the old navy/marigold/Fraunces "field journal" look (anti-reference).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The volunteer year is a journey, so the page is one Indian Railways reservation ticket for it; applying is booking. Refuses the NGO default of hero photo, stat row, card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Coach-blue ground (ICF blue, committed, ~50% of page) with ticket stock in pale thermal pink, near-blue-black ink, signal red for the single accent and stencil yellow for platform and coach signage only. Barlow Condensed signage caps, Hind for Devanagari and body, Doto dot-matrix for printed ticket fields. Perforations, notch cut-outs, stamped boxes, hairline rules, route-line stations. No cream, no serif, no cards-of-icons.
STORY: Visitor sees they can board a real year-long journey across India with a trusted institution, believes it from real photos and numbers, and books (Apply for a Visit).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: Gyan-Setu headline in signage caps with Devanagari, one short line, primary Apply for a Visit and secondary Learn More. Right: a tall ticket at large scale, field photo as its window cycling through destinations (Sukma, Ramban, Majuli, Ladakh, Mon, Tinsukia), printed route, passenger, class, validity fields, perforated stub. No stats strip in the hero.
FORM: Railway reservation ticket, dealt as the Pick (position 1 of 7 grounded candidates: rail ticket, State Transport destination board, Warli, textile block print, postal stamp sheet, school chart paper, chalkboard); seed key a8b6d31b.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
