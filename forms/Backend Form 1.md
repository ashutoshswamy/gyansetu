Educational Activity Research Centre - School Profile

This form is used to collect standardized information about schools participating in Educational Activity Research Centre (EARC) projects. The information provided will support project planning, implementation, monitoring, reporting, and impact assessment. Please ensure all details are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
